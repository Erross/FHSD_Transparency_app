PUBLIC SOCIAL-MEDIA ARCHIVE

FHSD Transparency Archive

A searchable historical record of prominent public social-media activity relating to Francis Howell School District.

Independent archive. Not affiliated with Facebook, FHSD, any candidate, campaign, or archived page. “Missing” means not observed; it does not by itself establish deletion or who caused it.

Raw captures are preserved with SHA-256 hashes. The archive distinguishes what was observed from claims about why content changed or disappeared.
