HOW TO READ THIS

Evidence, not attribution

The archive preserves raw snapshots and tracks stable post/comment identities through time.

  • Edited: same entity ID, different text.
  • Missing once: absent from one later complete crawl.
  • Missing on recheck: absent repeatedly.
  • Confirmed unavailable: separate direct/manual verification recorded.
  • Reappeared: a missing item became visible again.
